# BajetJe: Product Foundation

Full, editable version lives in Claude Docs ("BajetJe: Product Foundation"). This is the build copy.

BajetJe turns one salary into a clear monthly plan for Malaysian first-jobbers: what lands in the bank,
what they can spend each day, whether their commitments are healthy, and what car or home they can afford.
No tracking, no login, nothing leaves the phone.

## Purpose
- Vision: every Malaysian starts their working life knowing exactly what their salary can do.
- Mission: turn a payslip into a plan in under a minute, without tracking, accounts or jargon.
- Problem: first-jobbers guess, copy friends, or sign loans their life can't carry; trackers demand daily logging and get abandoned.

## Who it's for
- Primary: first-jobbers, years 0-3, RM 2,000-5,000 gross, urban Malaysia (e.g. Aiman, 23, KL, RM 3,500).
- Secondary: people changing salary; people about to sign a car loan or buy a first home.
- Not for v1: expense tracking, bank sync, investing, shared couple budgets.

## Positioning
For Malaysian first-jobbers who don't know what their salary allows, BajetJe is the salary planner that
tells you what you can afford in one minute. Unlike expense trackers, it never asks you to log a purchase,
and your salary never leaves your phone.

## Principles (in priority order)
1. Answer first  2. No tracking, ever  3. Honest over flattering (warn, never block)
4. Show the maths  5. Malaysian by default  6. Private by design  7. Calm, not gamified

## v1 scope
Salary (required, with payday) · My Plan hub · Commitments · Commitment Health · Car · Housing (with first-home eligibility).
Next: commitments grouped by needs/wants/savings with visible overflow; "try a new commitment" sandbox.
Later: weekend payday rule, joint income.

## Privacy
On-device encrypted storage, no account, no backend database. Public rates file comes down; nothing personal goes up.
Anonymous analytics events only. Export/import for phone changes. Target store label: "Data not collected".

## Brand
Name BajetJe. Taglines: "Masuk gaji je, bajet siap." / "Gaji in. Plan out."
Voice: a calm, honest older sibling who's good with money. Plain, honest, local, kind.

## Success metrics
North star: users who reach their plan and return on their next payday.
Inputs: salary-to-plan 80%, median time to plan < 60 s, commitments added 40%, payday return 35%,
car/home check used 30%, "I'd change a decision" 1 in 3. Starting hypotheses; reset after 500 users.

## Roadmap (gated, no dates)
1 Validate (interviews, prototype test; gate: 1 in 3 would change a decision)
2 Build v1 (engine + tests, 6 screens; gate: numbers match KWSP, PERKESO, LHDN)
3 Beta (100-500 users; gate: 80% reach plan, median < 60 s)
4 Launch and grow (gate: payday return 35%+ before monetising)
