# BajetJe: Product Foundation (build copy)

BajetJe turns one salary into a clear monthly plan for Malaysian first-jobbers: what lands in the bank, what they can spend each day,
whether their commitments are healthy, and what car or home they can afford. No tracking, no login, nothing leaves the phone.

- Vision: every Malaysian starts their working life knowing exactly what their salary can do.
- Users: first-jobbers, years 0-3, RM 2,000-5,000 gross, urban Malaysia. Not for v1: expense tracking, bank sync, investing.
- Principles: 1 Answer first · 2 No tracking, ever · 3 Honest over flattering (warn, never block) · 4 Show the maths ·
  5 Malaysian by default · 6 Private by design · 7 Calm, friendly, a little playful.
- Privacy: encrypted on-device storage, no account, no backend database. Rates file comes down; nothing personal goes up.
  Anonymous events only. Export/import for phone changes. Target store label "Data not collected".
- Brand: name BajetJe; Sketch B logo (hand-drawn, lime shades); doodle icons and illustrations; friendly, open voice in EN and
  BM santai with playful reactions ("Kira jap...", "Uish, kaya gila!", "Banyak lagi ni!"). Tagline "Masuk gaji je, bajet siap."
- North star: users who reach their plan and return on their next payday. Roadmap: Validate -> Build v1 -> Beta -> Launch (gated).
