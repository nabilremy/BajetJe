# Bundled fonts

All three are licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
Latin subsets downloaded from Google Fonts so the app makes no font request at runtime.

- Geist (variable 400 to 700): https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist
- JetBrains Mono (variable 400 to 700): https://fonts.google.com/specimen/JetBrains+Mono
- Caveat Bold (700): https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Caveat
